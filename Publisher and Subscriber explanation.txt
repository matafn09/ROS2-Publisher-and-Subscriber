I choose to send the location of an object (coordinates) to a subscriber using a publisher (emisor) /draw_circle and /pose_subscriber as a receiver.
The idea is that for every x,y position determined in the subscriber, the receiver will print the exact x,y positions of this object.

This is specifically good for robots because with this, we can see the real-time position of it as it moves throughout a space, helping us to keep 
a precise location of the robot in case human checking is not available.
